Lint is fuel. A restricted vent makes the dryer run hotter and longer, which is bad for safety and for your utility bill.

## Warning signs
- Clothes still damp after a full cycle
- Burning smell near the dryer
- Outdoor flap stuck shut or packed with lint
- Dryer stops mid-cycle from overheating
