A “quote after we look” visit is how most dryer-vent companies still work. We publish the price first so you can decide before anyone rolls a truck.

## What’s in the flat rate
When the job matches the tier you booked, the visit includes:

- Full clean from the dryer connection out to the exterior termination
- Basic inspection of the duct and outdoor cap
- Airflow check when the clean is done
- Before-and-after photos of the duct
- Cleanup of the work area

You pick the tier online from two questions (exit type and run length). Standard ground / side-wall jobs are **$129**. Roof, upper-floor, or long runs are **$169**.

## What is not in the flat rate
We do not bury add-ons at the door for ordinary access. The flat rate does **not** cover:

- New duct, cap, or dryer replacement parts
- Full HVAC duct cleaning (this is dryer vent only)
- Same-day emergency response without a confirmed opening
- Work outside Boulder County service towns we list on the site

If access is materially different from what you described (for example a sealed chase we can’t reach without cutting), we stop and talk before doing extra work.

## How booking works
Request a visit online. We confirm a **2-hour window** within one business day (usually a few business days out while we ramp). Pay after the visit — Venmo is fine.
