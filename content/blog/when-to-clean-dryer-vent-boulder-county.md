Most homeowners wait until clothes take forever to dry. That’s late.

## A simple rule
If it’s been **12 months** since a full clean — or you don’t remember the last one — schedule it. Move sooner if dry times stretch, the dryer feels hotter than usual, or you see lint at the outdoor cap.

## Why Boulder County homes clog
Long dryer runs, cold-weather heavier loads, and townhome/stacked laundry setups all pack lint. Side-wall exits are common here; roof exits need different access and pricing.

## What a proper clean includes
Dryer-to-exterior path cleaned, basic inspection, airflow check, before/after photos, and cleanup — at a **fixed published price** when access matches what you booked.
